Who we are

MJ Recruit is a hospitality staffing agency based in Leicester. Our clients run the food and drink at the UK's biggest events, and we supply the crews that keep their units moving.

We know events Our crews have worked Creamfields, Download Festival, Trent Bridge, Donington Park and Warwick Castle. Grills, tills, coffee units and bars.

We know our people Our coordinators work the same events as our crews, so we know exactly who we're sending and what the job takes.

We get them there Our own drivers collect crews from pickup points across Leicester and deliver everyone to the gate together. Nobody late, nobody lost, nobody missing.
