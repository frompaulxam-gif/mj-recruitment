# Product

## Register

brand

*(The marketing site is the primary surface — brand register. The `/app` run-sheet calculator is product register; treat it as app UI when working on it.)*

## Users

- **The owner of MJ Recruitment** (decision-maker): sees this first on a Friday Zoom pitch. Needs to instantly feel "this makes us look like a real company" and "this saves Dee hours".
- **Client bookers** (caterers/concessionaires like D&J Catering and Sanjay; venues like Warwick Castle): office context, desktop or phone, comparing staffing suppliers. Job: "get me 15 reliable staff for the 27th, delivered to site."
- **Workers** (Leicester-based, mostly 18–25, students and side-jobbers): apply from their phones, low patience for forms. Job: "get on the books, see dates, get picked up, get paid."
- **Dee** (coordinator, daily operator): runs everything from her phone inside WhatsApp. Job: turn an availability list into cars, times and a message she can paste.

## Product Purpose

MJ Recruitment supplies event/catering staff across the UK (clients: D&J Catering, Sanjay; sites like Trent Bridge, Donington Park, Warwick Castle) but has zero web presence — a dead domain and a Facebook page. Ops run entirely on WhatsApp with manual tallying and car-planning.

This project = (1) a marketing site that wins new caterer/venue clients and takes worker applications, (2) a phone-first "run sheet" calculator that turns a ticked staff list into cars, pickup times and a paste-ready WhatsApp message. Success: the owner says yes to a paid build on the Friday call; Dee actually uses the calculator.

## Brand Personality

**Grafting, dependable, early-morning.** The brand of a crew that's loading in at dawn while everyone else sleeps. Hi-vis amber on dark tarmac, road-sign typography, plain talk. Confidence through operational competence — pickup points, run sheets, staff counts — not corporate polish.

## Anti-references

- Corporate recruitment-agency template: navy + teal, stock photos of handshakes and headsets, "PEOPLE. PASSION. PERFORMANCE."
- SaaS landing slop: cream bg, gradient text, hero-metric grids, eyebrow labels on every section.
- Anything that feels like it replaces WhatsApp or automates Dee away — the tools are "Dee's assistant".
- Plastic AI look; fake logos for real clients (use text names only).

## Design Principles

1. **Show the operation, don't claim values.** Real pickup points, real venues, real numbers beat adjectives.
2. **Phone-first always.** Dee's phone is the primary device; every screen must work one-handed at 375px.
3. **The message is the product.** The calculator's output must match Dee's exact WhatsApp format — @tags, points, times — copy-paste ready.
4. **Low-friction forms.** Worker application ≤ 1 minute, D&J-style fields only (licence + transport questions included).
5. **Amber means action.** Hi-vis amber reserved for CTAs, drivers, and the things that move — never decoration wallpaper.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1; amber fills carry near-black text (hi-vis semantics), navy fills carry white.
- Reduced-motion alternatives for all animation.
- Plain English throughout — many workers speak English as a second language; forms use short labels, no jargon.
