MJ online, in three phases

You already own mjevents.co.uk (paid up to 2028, it just needs a site on it). D&J paid a web agency for their system. This is yours, built by someone who's stood at the pickup point at 6:30am.

Phase 1 ready now, you've just seen it

Website + run sheet tool

  • Client side: "get availability" enquiry form, so new caterers and venues can find and book MJ
  • Worker side: apply in under a minute, licence & car captured up front
  • Dee's run sheet: tick the crew and it builds the cars, times and the WhatsApp message, pickups and drop-offs both
  • Live on mjevents.co.uk within 2 weeks of a yes

Phase 2 next

Availability link + auto chase-ups

  • Dee posts one link per date-set: workers tap the dates they can do; the tally builds itself
  • Night-before reminders with 👍 tracking, so silence gets flagged before it becomes a no-show
  • Backup list: anyone not confirmed by the cut-off (say midnight) is dropped and the next standby gets a text to step in. No more finding out at the pickup point

Phase 3 later

Timesheets + staff portal

  • Hours & timesheets per event: names to the client and pay-ready numbers in one place
  • Staff portal, no passwords: workers log in with their phone number, a one-time text code and a 4-digit PIN. Forgotten PINs reset by us, not by Dee

Growth how the site pays for itself

Getting new clients

  • Reviews engine: after every event, ask the client for a Google review and a referral, then show the best ones on the site. Word of mouth closes better than any advert
  • Target list: every caterer, concessionaire and venue in the Midlands pulled from Google Maps, then a personal call with one line: we supply crews and we deliver them to site ourselves
  • The form qualifies: enquiries arrive with numbers, dates and urgency already answered, so the call back is a close, not a fact-find
  • Overdeliver early: a new client's first booking gets the strongest crew and a check-in call. Happy first order, repeat client
